# Acukoto CPU: fused multiply-adds in the body — 2026-11-09

Acukoto 0.25.7 against 0.25.6 (`f8e66c4`). One change: on CPUs with AVX2 and
FMA3 the body's modal step and the junction's two matrix products use fused
multiply-adds. A CPU with AVX2 alone keeps 0.25.6's exact AVX2 kernels, and
every other CPU the portable ones. No mode, coordinate, coefficient, control
period or level changed.

## Where the time goes

Callgrind on `AcukotoCpuProbe --preset 0 --oversampling 1 --scenario dense`
(0.3 s, only `AcukotoEngine::process` collected, `-O3 -g`): the modal step is
51% of the engine's instructions. Its drive from the 26 bar coordinates and
its pull back on them, 26 × 356 float products each per sample, are 24% on
their own lines and about 33% with their loop overhead and reductions; the
mode recursion is about 7% and the radiation sums 2%. The junction's two
26 × 26 products are 6%. The 52 string loops, the hand, the room and the
control-rate updates are the remaining 42%.

0.25.6's kernels are compiled `no-fma` so that they match the portable bank
to the bit, which costs each product a separate multiply and add. The fused
kernels (`driveModesFma`, `pullOnBarFma`, `runModesFma`, `transposeApplyFma`)
keep every sum's order and lanes and round once per term instead of twice.

## Measured CPU

GCC 13.3, Release (`-O3`), on a shared 4-vCPU Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz
container. `AcukotoCpuProbe --oversampling 1 --seconds 2 --scenario all`,
48 kHz, 64-frame callbacks, thirteen strings, two alternating
before/after pairs. Calling-thread CPU per callback, median and 99th
percentile over both runs (3000 blocks a case); a 64-frame block is
1333 µs. Development-machine figures only, never Reason or DAW CPU.

| Case | 0.25.6 median, µs (% of a core) | 0.25.7 median | Median reduction | 0.25.6 p99, µs | 0.25.7 p99 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hirajoshi, dense | 187 (14.0%) | 168 (12.6%) | 10.2% | 272 | 233 |
| Hirajoshi, gestures | 205 (15.4%) | 183 (13.7%) | 11.0% | 314 | 308 |
| Hankumoijoshi, dense | 159 (11.9%) | 143 (10.7%) | 10.2% | 216 | 371 |
| Hankumoijoshi, gestures | 189 (14.2%) | 156 (11.7%) | 17.8% | 353 | 243 |

The medians fell in every case in both pairs, by 9-21% per pair. The 99th
percentiles did not move dependably: on this shared machine they follow other
tenants more than the engine (one 0.25.7 Hankumoijoshi dense run had a p99
of 184 µs, the other 414 µs, with the same code and input). Worst-case blocks
need a quiet machine to judge.

Clang 18 builds of both versions, two more pairs run while the unit tests
used another core: the first pair was mixed (−12% to +21% by case), the
second 10-16% lower in every case. Clang's null test matches GCC's: the
residual is 135-139 dB under the signal, its peak −135 dBFS at most.

The project's goal of under 10% of a core on worst-case blocks at 1× is not
met: the medians are 10.7-13.7% and the 99th percentiles 17-28% here.

## The sound

A null test on the same runs: the probe's raw float output (`--samples`), all
four cases, 2 s each, 0.25.7 minus 0.25.6.

| Case | Signal RMS | Residual RMS | Residual peak | Residual under the signal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hirajoshi, dense | −22.5 dBFS | −158.0 dBFS | −141.0 dBFS | 135.5 dB |
| Hirajoshi, gestures | −17.1 dBFS | −155.2 dBFS | −138.5 dBFS | 138.1 dB |
| Hankumoijoshi, dense | −22.4 dBFS | −158.3 dBFS | −141.0 dBFS | 135.9 dB |
| Hankumoijoshi, gestures | −16.9 dBFS | −155.6 dBFS | −138.5 dBFS | 138.7 dB |

The largest difference is one 24-bit step (−138.5 dBFS), the rounding of the
float sums and nothing more: the change is inaudible by construction and by
measurement, so no listening test was run. The output is no longer the same
to the bit on every x86 CPU: an AVX2 CPU without FMA3 renders 0.25.6's bits,
one with FMA3 these.

`Acukoto.ModalRadiation` keeps its bit-exact checks of the AVX2 kernels and
adds the fused ones: over every bank size, with and without a strike, each
output meets the portable bank within 1e-4 of its vector's largest
magnitude (1e-12 for the double-precision junction). A fused drive with one
coordinate left out fails it.
